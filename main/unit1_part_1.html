Unit 1. Part 1.

Read and translate the text. About myself.

Hello, friends. My name is Nurlan. My surname or last name is Saduakasov. I was born on November 17 in 2002 in Kostanai region, Auliekol district, Aman-Karagai village.

Two years ago, I finished secondary school and decided to enter Kostanai Engineering and Economic University named after Myrzhakyp Dulatov.

At school, I did well in all the subjects but my favorite subjects were Math, Physics and Physical Education.

I am fond of travelling very much and getting to know something new. However, most of all I appreciate Computer Science, that is why, I have chosen “Computing technology and software” specialty.

My father is a businessperson and he wanted me enter the faculty of Economics. Nevertheless, I decided to be an IT specialist. I believe that in future I will help to develop my father’s business using my professional IT skills.

The other areas of study at the university are the following:

  • automation and control system engineering,
  • mechanical engineering
  • transportion management, transport equipment and technologies
  • information systems
  • agricultural machinery and technology
  • standardization and certification (by industry) and other.

Besides, I am very interested in learning English because English is the language of world communication. Mastering and fluency in English language is directly related to career opportunities, relocation abroad and higher salaries. Moreover I hope I will be able to get the ability to read professional literature, technical manuals and specifications without a dictionary. English will help me to get in close communication with foreign IT colleagues at forums, be successful in exchange of knowledge and experience in solving problems and challenges. So, in two years, I will graduate from the University and will work as an IT professional.

Now let me describe my appearance. I am tall and slim and have short dark hair and hazel eyes. I think I am a handsome man. I love sports and music. I love to listen to modern music and dance. I also love swimming and hand-to-hand fighting.

Now I would like to say you a few words about my family. There are five people in our family: a father, a mother, a grandmother, a younger sister and me.

My father is 50 years old; he is a businessperson. My mother is 48 years old; she is a schoolteacher. She teaches Biology and Chemistry and has much work about the house because I have a younger sister Saule. Saule is a pupil; she is in the fifth form. My grandmother lives with us. She is very kind and helps us a lot.

Our family is very friendly, caring and hospitable. Therefore we like to spend time together, take care of each other and are fond of inviting guests. I love my family very much.
